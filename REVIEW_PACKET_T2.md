# Operational Control Room Intelligence Environment

## 1. System Objective

The objective of this project is to transform operational signals into actionable decision intelligence.

The system simulates a miniature Operational Control Room capable of:

* Prioritizing operational signals
* Correlating signals into incidents
* Escalating incidents through organizational hierarchy
* Reconstructing operational timelines
* Supporting executive visibility
* Generating automated operational summaries

The system is fully deterministic and explainable with no machine learning components.

# 2. Signal Prioritization Logic

## Purpose

Operational signals were enriched with business context to support operational decision-making.

## Input

## Output

## Signal Attributes Added

Each signal was assigned:

* Severity
* Impact Area
* Operational Owner
* Resolution Target
* Operational Consequence

Example:

Demand Spike

* Severity: High
* Impact Area: Grid Demand
* Owner: Regional Controller

* Consequence: Potential grid stress due to elevated demand

# 3. Incident Correlation Logic

## Purpose

Operational incidents are generated by correlating multiple signals occurring at the same timestamp.

## Incident Generation Rules

Examples:

Demand Spike + Supply Stress

→ GRID_STRESS_INCIDENT

Forecast Failure + Demand Spike

→ FORECAST_RISK_INCIDENT

Low Renewable Window + High Carbon Period

→ SUSTAINABILITY_INCIDENT

## Incident Attributes

Each incident includes:

* Incident ID
* Timestamp
* Incident Type
* Contributing Signals
* Severity
* Business Impact
* Recommended Response
* Escalation Path

# 5. Timeline Logic

## Purpose

Operational events were reconstructed into a chronological timeline.

Each incident generates three events:

1. Incident Created
2. Escalated
3. Resolved

Resolution events are generated two hours after escalation.
