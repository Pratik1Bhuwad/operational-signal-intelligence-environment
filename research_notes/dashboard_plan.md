# Dashboard Design Plan

## Page 1: Executive Overview

KPIs:

* Average National Demand
* Peak National Demand
* Renewable Generation Share
* Forecast Accuracy
* Average Carbon Intensity

Visuals:

* KPI Cards
* Monthly Demand Trend

Signals:

* Demand Spike
* Forecast Failure

* High Carbon Period

Visuals:

* Signal Occurrence Counts
* Signal Timeline
* Monthly Signal Distribution

## Page 3: Explainability

Visuals:

* Fossil vs Renewable during High Carbon Periods
* Forecast Error Distribution
* Demand During Spike Events
* Carbon Intensity Analysis

Purpose:
Provide operational visibility and explain signal drivers.
